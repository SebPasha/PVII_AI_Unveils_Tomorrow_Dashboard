The dashboard

Every country, its plan and its budget: coverage by plan cycle, the SDG and HDI frameworks, the money behind each strategy, and the plan paragraphs behind each one. Every figure opens the arithmetic that produced it.

Mapping review

One budget line at a time, its candidate plan strategies on the left and the line in its place in the budget on the right. This is the exercise behind the precision figure.

Recall review

Sampled budget lines including the ones the model matched to nothing. A link a person finds that the model did not is a measured miss, which is the figure no model-side bound can give.

Sector strategy gaps

Where a national plan is silent on a risk, whether a sector strategy takes it up. Carries no money and is never compared across countries.
